Purpose:
• Provides detailed visibility into the criteria contributing to Supplier Engagement Coverage and identifies supplier populations requiring additional action.
What this indicator shows:
• Overall Supplier Engagement Coverage.
• Progress against the defined target.
• Supplier email/contact availability.

• Supporting criteria contributing to the overall indicator.

How to use:

INDICATOR 2 – RO READINESS / ONBOARDING PLANNING
Use the exact title from your Summary page when you paste this.
Purpose:
• Provides visibility into Relationship Owner readiness and the supporting onboarding/planning activities required to support supplier engagement.
What this indicator shows:
• Overall RO readiness position.
• RO onboarding/readiness activity status.
• Supporting criteria contributing to the indicator.
• Attendance/completion information from the applicable RO sessions.
• Outstanding RO actions or populations requiring follow-up.
How to use:
• Review the overall indicator and its supporting criteria to understand the current RO-readiness position.
• Use the detailed breakdown to identify outstanding onboarding or readiness activities.
• Review the underlying RO/supplier population where additional follow-up is required.
• Export the supporting list where further offline review or action tracking is required.
Data Note:
• Attendance/readiness information reflects the latest applicable RO onboarding and Supplier Impact Guide session records incorporated into the reporting.
