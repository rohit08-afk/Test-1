Indicator 1 – Supplier Engagement Coverage
Purpose
Confirm that each supplier can be reached and has a clear ExxonMobil Relationship Owner responsible for the relationship.
Proposed logic
A supplier will be counted as covered only when both conditions are met:
Communication sent successfully – using Comms Progress = Initial Comms Sent as the reachability proxy.
RO assigned – a Relationship Owner is populated for the supplier.
KPI logic
Comms Sent + RO Assigned = Supplier Engagement Coverage
This is an AND condition, not an average of the two components.
Why this works
It aligns with the readiness framework definition of having a validated deliverable email and an internal Relationship Owner, while using data already available in the dashboard.
Target
For High Priority suppliers: 100% by Aug 31.
Landing page view
The landing page will show only the high-level indicator:
Actual %
Target %
Target date
More Details button
More Details vision
Clicking More Details will open a dedicated indicator view showing:
Email Reachability
RO Assigned
Suppliers missing email/comms
Suppliers missing RO assignment
Suppliers missing both
Supplier-level list for follow-up
Overall intent
The landing page answers: “Are we on track?”
The detail view answers: “What is driving the gap, and which suppliers need action?”


Indicator 2 – RO Readiness Action Planning
Purpose
Confirm that Relationship Owners are prepared to support suppliers through the readiness process and understand the required onboarding, Supplier Impact Guide, roles and responsibilities, and approved BRT content.
Proposed logic
We can use the RO training / attendance sheet as the primary evidence source for this indicator.
An RO would be considered ready when the required onboarding / training requirement has been completed.
Potential completion evidence could include:
attendance at the required RO onboarding / Supplier Impact Guide session
approved self-learning completion, where applicable
any other agreed evidence that Robbie and Sonya confirm should count as proof of readiness
KPI logic
Required RO onboarding / training complete = RO Readiness
The exact completion rule still needs to be confirmed with Robbie and Sonya.
Why this works
The attendance sheet gives us a direct and auditable way to track whether required ROs have completed the readiness preparation expected by the framework, instead of relying on a subjective assessment.
Target
The readiness framework currently shows:
100% required RO onboarding by Sep 10
100% required RO Supplier Impact Guide orientation / approved self-learning by Sep 17
Landing page view
The landing page would show:
Actual %
Target %
Target date
More Details button
More Details vision
Clicking More Details could show:
ROs who attended the required session
ROs who completed an approved self-learning route
ROs still pending
readiness status by RO
suppliers assigned to ROs who are not yet ready
This would help explain whether the gap is due to missed attendance, incomplete self-learning, or another readiness requirement.
Questions to confirm with Robbie and Sonya
Can attendance at the required RO training / Supplier Impact Guide session be treated as the main readiness evidence?
If an RO does not attend, can approved self-learning also count as complete?
Are there any other ways we should use to confirm that RO onboarding and training is complete?
Should the KPI be measured by ROs ready or by suppliers covered by ready ROs?
Overall intent
The landing page answers: “Are the required ROs ready?”
The detail view answers: “Who is still pending, and what readiness requirement is incomplete?”
