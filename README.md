Indicator 3 – Supplier Acknowledgment Rate
Purpose
Confirm that the supplier has acknowledged the change, the correct supplier contact has been identified, and the supplier has engaged with the readiness process.
Proposed logic
A supplier would be counted as acknowledged when there is valid evidence of acknowledgment through one of the approved routes referenced in the readiness framework, such as:
Email confirmation
Verbal confirmation during a call / F2F discussion
Webinar attendance
Initial supplier conversation completed
The framework also indicates that the appropriate supplier/function contact should be confirmed.
KPI logic
Valid supplier acknowledgment + correct contact confirmed = Supplier Acknowledgment
The exact evidence routes and source field(s) still need to be confirmed with Robbie and Sonya.
Why this works
It aligns with the readiness framework, which distinguishes simple reachability from actual supplier engagement. Indicator 1 confirms that the supplier can be reached; Indicator 3 confirms that the supplier has actively acknowledged the change and entered the readiness process.

95% by Sep 30
100% by Oct 15

Indicator 2 – RO Readiness Action Planning
Purpose
Confirm that Relationship Owners are prepared to support suppliers through the readiness process and understand the required onboarding, Supplier Impact Guide, roles and responsibilities, and approved BRT content.
Proposed logic
We can use the RO training / attendance sheet as the primary evidence source for this indicator.
An RO would be considered ready when the required onboarding / training requirement has been completed.
Potential completion evidence could include:
attendance at the required RO onboarding / Supplier Impact Guide session
approved self-learning completion, where applicable
any other agreed evidence that Robbie and Sonya confirm should count as proof of readiness
KPI logic
Required RO onboarding / training complete = RO Readiness
The exact completion rule still needs to be confirmed with Robbie and Sonya.
Why this works
The attendance sheet gives us a direct and auditable way to track whether required ROs have completed the readiness preparation expected by the framework, instead of relying on a subjective assessment.
Target
The readiness framework currently shows:
100% required RO onboarding by Sep 10
100% required RO Supplier Impact Guide orientation / approved self-learning by Sep 17

The landing page would show:

Target %

More Details vision

ROs who attended the required session
ROs who completed an approved self-learning route
ROs still pending
readiness status by RO
suppliers assigned to ROs who are not yet ready
This would help explain whether the gap is due to missed attendance, incomplete self-learning, or another readiness requirement.

Should the KPI be measured by ROs ready or by suppliers covered by ready ROs?

The landing page answers: “Are the required ROs ready?”
The detail view answers: “Who is still pending, and what readiness requirement is incomplete?”
